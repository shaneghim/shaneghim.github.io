About

I'm building toward a career in property and casualty insurance. These projects use public insurance data to work through the problems actuaries and underwriters handle every day: estimating unpaid claims, modeling claim frequency, analyzing catastrophe losses, and measuring risk.

Projects

Loss Reserving: Chain Ladder vs. Bornhuetter-Ferguson

Excel · CAS Schedule P data

Paid loss triangles for a large auto insurer, backtested against actual outcomes. BF stayed within 3% for the most recent accident years, while chain ladder overestimated 2007 by 7.5%.

Auto Claim Frequency: Poisson GLM vs. GAM

R · about 678,000 motor liability policies

Frequency models tested on a 20% holdout. The riskiest 10% of policies had about six times the claim rate of the safest 10%.

U.S. Flood Insurance Claims Dashboard

SQL · Power BI · 2.7 million FEMA claims

A SQL to Power BI pipeline and interactive dashboard. Three hurricanes made up 37.7% of all payments, and claims in moderate to low-risk zones averaged close to those in high-risk zones.

California Wildfire Non-Renewal Analysis

Python · GIS · CDI and CAL FIRE data

Non-renewal data for about 1,800 ZIP codes joined to fire hazard zones. Insurer-initiated non-renewals in high-hazard areas rose 66% after the 2017 and 2018 fire seasons.

Bond Portfolio Interest Rate Risk Model

Excel · FRED yield data

A $6M Treasury portfolio stress-tested under nine rate scenarios. Adding convexity to duration kept the estimation error under 0.5 points.
